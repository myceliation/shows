Coolshows Atlas

A map and calendar of DIY shows in New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania. Listings come from coolshows.life and several venue calendars, and are checked every morning.

How it works and sources

Using the atlas

Pick a date on the calendar. The map and list show what is on around that date. Marker colors show how many days each show is from that date. Set a starting point to color shows by distance from you instead.

A dashed marker means the exact spot is not known or not public, so the marker sits at the center of the town. Many DIY shows happen in homes; when a listing says to message the organizer for the address, the atlas says so and does not guess a location.

Add shows to My plan to see the distance between them and to save them to your own calendar. The page address always links to what you are looking at, including your plan, so you can copy it from the address bar or with Copy link and send it to someone.

Sources

coolshows.life is the main source. Venue calendars add shows that were not sent to coolshows; when the same show appears in more than one place it is listed once with links to each. Distances are straight-line miles, not driving distance.
